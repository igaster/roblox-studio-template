# Scenario template: "a game like X"

Copy this to `.training/scenarios/<NNN>-<name>.md` (gitignored; the Lead is denied reading
`.training/`), or paste it into your message to the trainer, and fill it in.
The trainer reads it at session start; the Lead never sees it, only the brief below.

## Brief (sent to the Lead as the owner's words)

> I want to make a Roblox game like <X>: same kind of gameplay, but our own game with its own
> name, look and twist. Involve me only for <the selling point | GDD approval | each
> milestone plan>; decide everything else yourself. Stop after <M1>.
> [Optional:] Check in with me <at each playable checkpoint | halfway through each milestone>.

Leave the check-in line out for one headless turn per milestone. With it, the Lead pauses
mid-milestone, so you can steer the build before it ends.

## Owner policy (used by the trainer to answer the Lead)

- **Involvement level:** milestones
- **Stop after:** M1
- **Check-ins:** <none | each playable checkpoint | halfway through each milestone>
- **Playtests:** <maintainer plays at check-ins | nobody: the Lead judges from agent
  playtests, and "how does it feel" stays open for a human>
- **Selling point:** <the owner's pick, or "pick the Lead's recommendation">
- **Audience:** <e.g. 8–14, mobile-first, solo works well>
- **Pre-granted approvals:** <e.g. "approve the GDD and each milestone plan unless they add
  monetization or trading">
- **Always ask the maintainer:** <topics, or "none">
- **Everything else:** the Lead's call, marked `(assumed)`.
